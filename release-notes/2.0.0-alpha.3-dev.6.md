## Main changes since Alpha 3 Dev 2

- Dynamic Outdoor patching now keeps the complete build-local downstream `VIRTUAL-SKIN*` family aligned and also includes `cellular-emergency` when present.
- `VIRTUAL-SKIN-OVER-35C-TRIGGER` remains explicitly excluded.
- Sensor, threshold-array, and value counts are derived from each build's local stock Thermal files instead of fixed `2/14`, `11/77`, `12/84`, or `13/91` assumptions.
- The verified Fix 5 Thermal core is wrapped by the newer fail-closed runtime-evidence policy and atomic stock-cache/overlay promotion path.
- Android-AWK portable fixed-precision formatting replaces the unsupported dynamic `%.*f` formatter.
- Action, OTA auto-switch, and pTune override changes materialize and validate first; requested settings are committed only after success.
- Unknown device/build tuples remain Stock-only until exact postboot evidence is recorded.
- Installer and Action output use compact validation summaries and best-effort responsive separators without forcing `/dev/tty` on KernelSU.
- The repo-only online incident collector captures stock sources, previous-boot logs, pstore, boot reason, active/staged hashes, and canonical validation state without being shipped in the flashable ZIP.

## Safety and rollback

- pTune coexistence remains experimental. Installed-disabled is safe; active pTune requires explicit acknowledgement.
- ZRAM 100p remains optional and requires explicit selection.
- Unknown device/build tuples remain Stock-only.
- To roll back, disable or remove the module in Magisk or KernelSU and reboot.
- This prerelease does not disable Android Thermal safety, replace the stock Thermal HAL, overclock the device, or provide a benchmark/FPS unlock.
